Demonstrated Accomplishments
What I Built & What I Delivered
P1 — ISO 27001 Compliance ProgramDeveloped an ISO 27001:2022 Annex A control matrix for a federal health IT contractor; assessed maturity across 20 controls, identified 4 critical gaps, and documented remediation owners with closure dates — producing a board-ready compliance baseline.
P2 — Enterprise Risk RegisterConstructed a 12-risk enterprise risk register using Likelihood × Impact scoring; built a color-coded heat map, tracked residual risk, and authored an executive summary prioritizing top 3 threats aligned to NIST RMF and ISO 31000.
P3 — Vendor Security AssessmentDesigned a 25-question TPRM questionnaire across 5 domains; assessed a cloud service provider controls and produced a risk scorecard (43/50 — Low Risk) with formal remediation recommendations.
P4 — Incident Response PlanAuthored a complete IRP per NIST SP 800-61 and ISO 27035; simulated a a ransomware tabletop simulation covering containment, eradication, recovery, and HIPAA breach notification requirements.
P5 — SOC 2 Cloud Control MappingMapped 15 SOC 2 TSC controls to a government cloud environment; documented implementations across audit logging, threat detection, access control, and security monitoring with mock audit evidence artifacts.
P6 — Interactive Compliance DashboardBuilt a live GRC dashboard with 6 tabbed views — control coverage, risk register, audit findings, vendor scorecard, and roadmap — translating raw GRC data into executive-level metrics.
P7 — Internal Audit & CAPPerformed a structured internal audit of 10 GRC controls (ISO 27001, SOC 2, HIPAA); identified 10 findings, produced a Corrective Action Plan with assigned owners and remediation timelines.
P8 — Tech Sector SOC 2 InternshipSimulated a 6-week SOC 2 Type II readiness engagement for a global technology company’s cloud services; scoped identity management, cloud storage, app distribution, and developer API services against AICPA TSC, producing risk register, control mapping, monitoring evidence binder, and executive readiness report.
P9 — Payment Network PCI DSS InternshipExecuted a 6-week PCI DSS compliance lifecycle for a global payment network; defined the Cardholder Data Environment (CDE), performed gap analysis, hardened API security with mTLS and OAuth 2.0, designed tokenization controls, and assembled a QSA evidence binder.
P10 — a telecommunications company ISO 27001 ISMS InternshipImplemented a full ISO 27001:2022 ISMS for a telecom company; defined scope, built risk register, drafted Statement of Applicability, implemented technical controls (MFA, encryption, IDS), conducted internal audit, and prepared the organization for Stage 1 certification audit.
P11 — TPRM Dual Sector InternshipConducted TPRM assessments across two sectors: A Major Healthcare Organization (HIPAA/HITRUST, ePHI, BAA review) and a national retail corporation (PCI DSS supply chain, payment processor due diligence) — producing vendor inventories, criticality matrices, remediation plans, KPI dashboards, and executive risk summaries.
